# Snug demo video: shot list

Target: 60 to 90 seconds, vertical 1080p, captions burned in. Most people watch muted, so every beat needs on-screen text.

Record the app with iOS screen recording from Control Center. Turn on Do Not Disturb first. Film the real-room shots on a second phone, or on the same phone between recordings.

| Time | Shot | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 s | Real footage: an empty bedroom wall, then a phone held up to it. | "Will this bed actually fit?" |
| 5 to 22 s | Screen recording: tap the room corners in AR. Cut the boring middle and keep the first tap and the room closing. | "Scan your room with any iPhone. No LiDAR needed." |
| 22 to 30 s | Screen recording: the floor plan review, dragging one corner to correct it. | "Fix anything the scan got wrong." |
| 30 to 45 s | Screen recording: open the catalog, add a real bed, then drag it toward a wall until the badge turns amber. | "Real products at their real size." Then: "Too close to call? Snug tells you to measure." |
| 45 to 55 s | Real footage: a tape measure across the same gap in the real room, matching the app. | "It was right." |
| 55 to 68 s | Screen recording: add a nightstand and dresser, orbit the room, tap "View on Amazon". | "Mix pieces from different brands. Buy the ones that fit." |
| 68 to 80 s | The before/after image, full screen. | "Share your before / after." |
| 80 to 90 s | End card: logo, the web demo link, and the GitHub link. | "Try it in your browser: <your link>" |

## Tips

- **The tape-measure shot carries the video.** It proves the honest fit check works in a real room. Get it in one clean take.
- **Show a "too close to call" moment.** An app that admits uncertainty is more memorable than one that only shows green checks.
- **Trim hard.** Any second with no change on screen is a second to cut. Under 75 seconds is better than 90.
- **Upload to YouTube as unlisted.** Embed the link in the README, the web demo, and your resume. Also save a GIF of the drag-into-wall moment for the README header.
